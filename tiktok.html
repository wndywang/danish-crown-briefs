01Why you

We picked you because your kitchen looks like a real kitchen and your followers trust you. This campaign is not about a perfect recipe video. It is about the morning you already make happen, every single day.

02The idea, in one word

Madiskarte.

The Filipino talent for making something out of almost nothing, and calling it pride rather than sacrifice. You do not need a full pantry. You take what you have, and you turn it into a moment. Tulip Jamonilla is not the hero of that story. You are. We just want to be in your kitchen while it happens.

10The short version of the rules

Please do

  • Hook us in the first 1.5 seconds.
  • Shoot in daylight, and make the food look good.
  • Keep the real kitchen sound in. The sizzle is half the story.
  • On-screen text in Tagalog. Talk the way you actually talk.
  • Show yourself tasting it and enjoying it.
  • Sensible kitchen hygiene on camera.

Please don't

  • No scripted, salesy lines. If you would not say it to a friend, cut it.
  • No other brands visible. Check your background.
  • No claims about the product beyond the four above.
  • No alcohol, cigarettes, nudity, or making fun of anyone.
  • Children preferably do not appear. If yours must, we need the brand to approve first.
  • No sustainability, climate or political talk.
