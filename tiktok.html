You write it

YOUR SCRIPT.

We are not sending you lines to read out. The whole point is that it sounds like you, so you write it and we approve it. We'll send a short template with blanks. It takes about twenty minutes.

One important thing: everything in English. The brand has asked for all content in English for this campaign, spoken and on screen. Keep it natural and conversational, exactly how you would say it to a friend.

Please do

  • Hook us in the first 1.5 seconds.
  • Daylight, and make the food look good.
  • Keep the real kitchen sound in. The sizzle is half the story.
  • Speak in English, but talk the way you actually talk. Natural, not formal.
  • Show yourself tasting it and enjoying it.
  • Sensible kitchen hygiene on camera.

Please don't

  • No scripted, salesy lines. If you wouldn't say it to a friend, cut it.
  • No other brands visible. Check your background.
  • No claims about the product beyond the four approved ones.
  • No alcohol, cigarettes, nudity, or making fun of anyone.
  • Children preferably don't appear. If yours must, the brand approves first.
  • No sustainability, climate or political talk.

One rule that protects everyone: don't start filming until your script is approved, so no shoot day gets wasted.
